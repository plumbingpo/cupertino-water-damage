Common Water Damage Issues in San Diego

Water usually escapes at the same few vulnerable spots in a home. The list below covers what we are asked to clean up most for San Diego owners.

Water heaters: Over the years, mineral deposits settle in a tank and corrode the metal underneath. The first sign is often a rusty puddle or a damp ring on the floor around the base. If the tank ruptures, it keeps refilling and draining onto the floor until someone closes the cold supply valve. Heaters in a hallway closet or attic can ruin flooring and ceilings well beyond the utility area.

Roofs: One strong storm is enough to peel back shingles and push water beneath the roof covering. Skylights, plumbing vents and chimneys are other entry points once their seals crack with age. Since water follows the framing downhill, the ceiling stain rarely sits directly below the actual opening. On San Diego roof leak calls, our crew removes saturated insulation, sets drying equipment and can cover the opening until a roofer arrives.

Freezing: Depending on the season, a hard overnight freeze can burst lines that were fine the day before. Outdoor spigots, sprinkler feeds and pipes in crawl spaces or along outside walls are hit first. Vacant homes and rooms with the heat turned down are especially exposed. No flow or a bare trickle at the tap on a frigid day means a frozen line, so close the main and get help.

Backups: A drain that gurgles or several fixtures that slow down together point to a blockage in the main line. Once it closes off fully, anything you flush or drain returns at the lowest opening in the house. Since the waste is hazardous, we extract it and then sanitize concrete, tile and other hard surfaces. Stay out of the affected rooms and avoid running water until the line is cleared.

Seepage: A wet line along the bottom of a foundation wall is a sign that water is building up in the soil next to it. The usual reasons are grading that tilts inward, clogged perimeter drains and downspouts that empty too close. White chalky deposits, peeling masonry paint and a persistent damp smell are early warnings. The team at Cupertino Restoration can extract standing water and dry the space, and we will tell you what we think let it in.
